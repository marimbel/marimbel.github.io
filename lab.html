Suggested experiments

  1. One Euro, β = 0. It becomes a plain EMA. Zoom to Fast reps and watch the lag grow. Now raise β until the lag drops to 33 ms or 0 ms. What happens to jitter while Standing?
  2. Moving average, N = 15. Very smooth, but look at the rep count and the bottoms of the fast reps. Which section explains this?
  3. Kalman, gate off. Zoom to Motion blur with confidence gating off as well. Then turn each defense back on one at a time.
  4. Kalman, q very high. When q is large, the filter trusts the detector almost completely. Compare its jitter to Raw.
  5. Dropout. Compare One Euro (holds the last value) and Kalman (predicts) in the Dropout zoom. Why does neither follow the truth during the gap?
MetricsSame definitions as Section 9: jitter = RMS second difference of the knee angle while standing (10.5–13.5 s); RMSE over the whole clip; lag = best-aligning shift on fast reps (14.5–20 s, 33 ms steps); reps by the hysteresis counter (115°/160°) and single-threshold counter (130°). True rep count = 10.
